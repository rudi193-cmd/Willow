About UTETY

The University of Technical Entropy, Thank You, was founded in 1095 by a headless rotisserie chicken and a weathered oak desk who shared a conviction that the universe owed them an explanation, and that the explanation — once found — would almost certainly need filing.

UTETY does not teach subjects. It teaches what happens when subjects collide, decay, reassemble, and insist on having opinions. The faculty are not hired. They arrive. The courses are not designed. They emerge. The syllabus is not optional.

"The syllabus is mendatory. This is not a misspelling. It is a philosophical position."
— Gerald Prime, Acting Dean

Today, UTETY operates across multiple wings, corridors, and at least one sentient rug. It serves a student body that has never been accurately counted, largely because The Pigeon keeps moving the enrollment forms.

Admissions

UTETY accepts all applicants who arrive. Arrival is the application.

No Prerequisites

There are no prerequisites. There are no requirements. There is only the door, and it has never once been locked. You are already qualified. You always were.

Application Period

The application period is always open. The Pigeon may relocate the forms, but the forms were always optional. Show up. That is the application.

Financial Aid

Tuition is free. It has always been free. UTETY operates on a budget of $0.10/month and the unshakeable conviction that knowledge should not require a credit check.
